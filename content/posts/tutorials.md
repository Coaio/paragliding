While the ability to soar high up into the sky is impressive, what really makes paragliding special is the combination of breathtaking footage and a perfectly curated soundtrack. Despite the peaceful feeling, it's important to remember that flying demands active focus at all times.

# Theo de blic

![Theo de Blic](/images/theo.jpg)

Theo de Blic is a five-time world cup champion in paragliding and an accomplished entrepreneur, professional pilot, and content creator. On his YouTube channel, he offers tutorials for intermediate pilots who want to learn advanced techniques in a progressive way. He records his instructional videos in-flight using new 360-degree cameras, providing a unique perspective that captures all the action. Whether you're an intermediate pilot looking to hone your skills or simply curious about the world of paragliding, Theo de Blic's channel offers a wealth of insights and inspiration that are not to be missed.
