**Windy** is a Czech company providing interactive [weather forecasting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_forecasting) services worldwide.

The portal was founded by [Ivo Lukačovič](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivo_Luka%C4%8Dovi%C4%8D) in November 2014. Currently, weather forecasts are based on data from the [GFS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Forecast_System) models, [ECMWF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Centre_for_Medium-Range_Weather_Forecasts), and NEMS model from the Swiss company [Meteoblue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meteoblue). Initially, the portal focused on wind animation, currently there are other basic meteorological parameters such as [temperature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperature), [pressure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pressure), [relative humidity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relative_humidity), cloud base and additional panels with more advanced data. The wind animation is based on the open sources project of [Cameron Beccario](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Cameron_Beccario&action=edit&redlink=1) *earth*
