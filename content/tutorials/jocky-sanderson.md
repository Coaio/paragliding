Jocky Sanderson is a BHPA Chief Flying Instructor based in the Lake District. He runs tandem flights, SIV courses over the water in Ölüdeniz, and one-to-one cross-country coaching.

The free teaching is on his YouTube channel. He says he is happy to share 35 years of paragliding experience there.
