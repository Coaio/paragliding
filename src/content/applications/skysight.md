SkySight is a soaring forecast. Set the activity to paragliding and the maps change to suit a slower wing. The useful view is the route forecast: draw the line you hope to fly and read a vertical slice of cloud base, wind, and where the thermals top out.

It is a subscription. XCTrack, XCSoar, and SeeYou Navigator can take a task from it, so the plan on the website can become the task on the instrument.

## What to look at

- Cloudbase and how deep the clouds are. A thick column can mean the day will overdevelop.
- The gap between trigger height and thermal top. A large gap is organised climb. A small one is scratching.
- Wind along the route, not only at home.
- A cross-section through a pass or a valley wind you have to cross.

Matthew Scutter, who builds it, has a long forecasting lesson in Cross Country Magazine. The tool is only half of the decision. The other half is whether you should be in the air at all.
