pgPilot puts a vario, a map, and airspace on the phone, and it keeps a place for the radio beside that. It runs on Android and iPhone. A Bluetooth vario can feed it altitude and wind. The map is meant to work after you have downloaded it, so a valley with no signal is still navigable.

It is a flying display, not a certified instrument. Look outside.

## On the map

- Climb, wind, and an FAI triangle assistant while you are still up.
- Airspace and NOTAMs, plus obstacle warnings.
- Live pilots from the Open Glider Network, FLARM, and Meshtastic.
- A side view along the direction you are flying.
- Weather measurements and a forecast on the same screen, so you can see whether the model matches the day.

Live tracking lets someone on the ground follow the flight. Set that up before you launch, and agree who is watching.
