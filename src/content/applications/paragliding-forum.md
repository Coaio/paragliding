Paragliding Forum is the English-language board pilots still use when a question does not fit in a Facebook group. Gear threads, site reports, and incident write-ups go back years. Search before you post. The useful answer is often already there, including the correction three posts later.

Read it as a conversation, not as instruction. A wing review is one pilot's weight range, climate, and habits. An incident report is worth more if you look for the decision that led to it, not for a verdict on the equipment.

The safety section is the place to start when you want other pilots' accounts of a particular failure. It does not replace your association's safety notice or the manufacturer's service bulletin.
