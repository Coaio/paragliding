Cross Country Magazine covers paragliding and hang gliding: wing reviews, competition reports, and lessons on weather and technique. The site mixes free articles with a subscription. The Fly Better series is the part worth reading on a rest day, including forecasting lessons that walk through SkySight and the columns Honza Rejmanek writes about the sky itself.

A review is a comparison on one day, in one place. Use it to learn what the test pilot was looking at, then read the certification report before you buy a wing.
