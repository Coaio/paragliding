DHV-XC is the online cross-country portal of the German Hang Gliding and Paragliding Association. You upload an IGC file from a flight instrument or an app. The server scores it, files it in your log, and shows it to anyone looking at the day's flights.

The German cross-country championship runs on it, with class lists, regional lists, and club lists. Pilots outside Germany use it as a public logbook. To score in the national championship you need a DHV membership number on the account. The Fun Cup is the list for pilots who are not in that championship.

Flyskyhy can send a track here directly. Sidekik cannot import DHV-XC flights yet. Keep your own copy of every IGC file.
