The Paragliding World Cup Association runs the World Cup, a season of cross-country competitions that sits alongside the FAI world championships. The site carries the calendar, the entry information, and the results.

Tasks and daily rankings move during an event. For the official status of a competition, and for a pilot's world ranking, check CIVL Comps as well. The two lists are not the same series.

Watching a task is a useful way to see how a route is chosen on a given day. It is not a lesson in how to fly that valley yourself.
