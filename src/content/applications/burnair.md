Burnair is a map built for free flight, used most in the Alps. One screen holds the wind at several heights, a thermal forecast, launch pages, airspace, and NOTAMs. Part of the map is free. Thermal detail and some layers need a subscription.

Use it the evening before, then again in the morning. A five-day overview tells you whether to keep the day free. The launch forecast tells you whether that hill is the one to drive to.

## What to read

- Wind at takeoff height and higher up. A valley wind and the gradient wind are often different.
- Rain and cloud, before you trust a thermal map.
- The launch page for local rules, landing fields, and cables.
- Airspace and the day's NOTAMs on the same view.

Burnair does not know the rotor behind your ridge. Pair it with a live station such as Holfuy or SpotAiR, and with someone who flies the site.
