CIVL Comps is the event and ranking site of the FAI Hang Gliding and Paragliding Commission. It lists sanctioned competitions for cross-country, accuracy, aerobatics, and hike-and-fly, and it holds the world pilot ranking that used to live on a separate FAI site.

A pilot who wants to enter an FAI category 2 event needs a CIVL ID. Register before the competition asks for it. The ranking page shows the points that make up a current rank, and which competitions they came from.

Organisers publish the event page, the entry list, and the results here. The rules of the competition are still the ones in the local regulations and in FAI Section 7. The website is the index, not the rule book.
