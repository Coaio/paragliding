Paragliding Map is the mobile version of [ParaglidingMap.com](https://www.paraglidingmap.com/). It is built for paraglider and hang glider pilots, and it is useful for other free-flight pilots who need the same sites and weather.

Open it before you leave home. The map mixes flying sites with forecasts, live stations, and webcams, so you can compare several hills instead of checking each one on a different website.

## What is on the map

The developer currently lists more than 15,000 launches, 7,500 landings, 30,000 weather stations, and 39,000 webcams. Site data comes from the DHV, Flyland, the FFVL, Paragliding Earth, and other official sources. Each site links back to the original page.

- Photos of takeoff and landing, and a 3D view.
- Hike-and-fly trails, plus ski lifts, cable cars, and gondolas.
- Valley-wind arrows for the European Alps.
- Wind animation for cloud, rain, storms, and wind at different heights.
- Airspace and NOTAMs on the map, with a warning when an active NOTAM affects your launch or landing.
- Filters for paragliding, hang gliding, speed flying, thermalling, soaring, hike-and-fly, and public transport.
- Site descriptions translated into English.
- Search by site, city, or region, and driving directions to the spot.
- Favorites, and a chat group on each launch for lifts, retrieves, and local advice.
- A time slider so you can see the map as the forecast thinks it will look later.

## Weather

Forecasts come from Meteoblue, Windy, and WindFinder, and the site also points at the UK Met Office, the German Weather Service, Météo-France, ECMWF, and NOAA. Live stations include Pioupiou, Holfuy, MADIS, and the FFVL. Treat the numbers as a briefing, then look at the sky when you arrive.

Many features are free and carry advertising. An in-app purchase turns the ads off and unlocks the paid layers, such as webcams and some forecasts.

You can also send an SOS or an "I need a ride" message to friends, with your GPS position. That is a convenience message, not a rescue beacon.
