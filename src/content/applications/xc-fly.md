XC Fly is a flight app for iPhone and Android. It records an IGC file, detects takeoff and landing, and shows a vario, altitude, speed, and a thermal assistant. The map carries airspace, obstacles, airports, and flying sites, and that map can be saved for offline use. A Bluetooth instrument can feed it.

It also covers paramotor flights, including engine data when a supported unit is connected. Live tracking and a group feed sit next to the logbook. In Brazil it shows official DECEA NOTAMs.

The basic app is free. A subscription adds more. It is still a phone. Load the airspace before you leave coverage, and keep a real lookout.
