SpotAiR is a map for weather-dependent outdoor flying, built first for paragliding and hang gliding. It pulls live wind stations, webcams, rain radar, and site information onto one screen, and it can save a region for offline use. Coverage is deepest in France, where it uses FFVL sites and beacons, and it also reads Paragliding Earth, Pioupiou, and METAR stations elsewhere.

The basic map is free. A Pro account adds more. Airspace can be filtered by floor and ceiling, which is the useful way to hide something that sits far above the day you are flying.

## Before you leave

- Open the launch and read the live wind, then the webcam, then the forecast you already looked at.
- Check the site status. Some sites are closed, limited to a rating, or outside a local agreement.
- Download the area if the valley has no signal.

A green station is not a clearance to launch. It is one measurement.
