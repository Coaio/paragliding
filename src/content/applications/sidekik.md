Sidekik is for paraglider pilots and clubs. You use it before the flight to pick a goal, during the flight to track and see the wind, and after landing to replay the day and share it. It is free to start on iPhone and Android. It does not replace a flight instrument.

The basic app is free. Extra features sit behind a monthly or yearly Plus subscription. A club can be created for free, with one challenge a year. More tasks and stats need the club subscription.

## Before you launch

Peak Hunts are lists of summits and landmarks in a flying area. Fly or walk through the circle around a peak and it counts. Harder summits are worth more, and some peaks count on foot, so a new pilot and an XC pilot can be in the same challenge.

Sidekik also scores uploaded flights on its own. You can see which summits you already have, which are still open, and medals for a mountain range: bronze, silver, gold, and diamond.

## In the air

Start in Fly or Hike & Fly. One recording can hold the walk up and the flight. While you walk, the screen shows height gain, pace, and climb. While you fly, it shows the variometer and XC distance.

- Thermals and Livewind on the map. Live wind is estimated from your circles.
- OpenAIP airspace, cables, and obstacles.
- A KK7 thermal layer for planning.
- A peak assistant that tells you how much height you still need for the next summit, and whether your glide reaches it.
- Live tracking so friends and clubmates can follow you, using XContest live tracking, the Open Glider Network, and Sidekik's own tracking.

## After landing

The hike and the flight are split and colored differently on the map. You get airtime, distance, elevation, photos, and XP. A 3D replay flies the track over the terrain. You can record a short video of that replay in the app.

## Bringing flights in

Connect XContest, Garmin Connect, or Suunto and Sidekik imports activities for you. You can also upload an IGC or GPX file from XCTrack, Flyskyhy, SeeYou Navigator, or a GPS vario.

Two limits are worth knowing. XContest flights have to be submitted to XContest on the day you flew, or Sidekik cannot import them later. DHV-XC has no connection yet.
