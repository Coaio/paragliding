Holfuy is a network of automatic weather stations. A station on a launch reports wind speed, gusts, and direction, and many also send a picture. The map is the check you do after the forecast and before you drive, then again from the car park.

A station tells you the wind at that mast. It does not tell you the rotor behind the ridge or the wind a few hundred meters up. Read the average and the gust, and look at the last hour rather than one sample.

Save the stations for the hills you actually fly. If a station has been quiet, treat the number as missing, not as calm.
