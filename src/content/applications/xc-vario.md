XC Vario is an Android vario from theFlightVario. It mixes the phone's barometer with the motion sensors so the tone reacts faster than a barometer-only app. The audio can be tuned. A phone in a pocket is still a phone: the sensor quality depends on the handset, and the battery runs down with the screen and GPS on.

Use it as a vario and a logger. It does not replace a look at the airspace before you take off.

## What to check on the ground

- Lift the phone and listen for how quickly the tone follows. A slow beep is a slow vario, whatever the label says.
- Confirm it keeps recording with the screen off.
- Read the IGC after a test walk, so you know the file is one you can upload.
