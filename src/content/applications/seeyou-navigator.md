SeeYou Navigator is the phone version of Naviter's flight software. It runs on Android and iPhone, records an IGC file, and sits next to a vario or an Oudie rather than replacing one. The basic app is free. Maps, weather layers, and some navigation tools are in-app purchases.

It is aimed at soaring as well as paragliding and hang gliding, so the final-glide math is there if you want it.

## In the air

- Airspace warnings, and a thermal assistant.
- A glide calculation toward a landing place.
- Live traffic from the Open Glider Network.
- Rain radar, and forecast layers from SkySight and TopMeteo when you are connected.
- Upload to an online contest after landing, and a logbook in SeeYou Cloud.

The layout can be rearranged, and the screen works in portrait or landscape. Read the airspace on the ground first. A warning on a phone is late if you have not looked at the map.
