Meteo-Parapente is a forecast made for paragliding, not a general weather site with a wind layer added on. It runs its own model and draws thermal maps, wind, cloud, and a sounding you can drop on the route you mean to fly.

The daily picture is there to brief from. Finer products sit behind an account. Check the model against a second source, such as Windy or SkySight, when the day matters.

## How to read a point

- Move the cursor to the launch, not to the nearest town.
- Read the wind at takeoff and at the height you hope to reach.
- Look at the sounding for an inversion, the cloud base, and whether the wind increases as you climb.
- Compare the afternoon with the morning. A good-looking 2 p.m. can be a blown-out 4 p.m.

The model does not know your site's venturi or the local rule about which direction is too much. Use it with a site guide.
