Flyskyhy is a flight instrument for iPhone. It shows altitude, climb, speed, glide angle, and a computed wind, and it draws a moving map of where you climbed and where you sank. A Bluetooth vario supplies the altitude and the vario tone. The phone on its own is a backup, not a certified logger.

The screens are built from pieces you can move, resize, and remove. Airspace and waypoints are extra purchases. Maps can be saved for a day with no signal.

## What it is for

- Finding a thermal you just left, because the map marks the lift.
- Checking whether a landing field is still in glide, with the wind included.
- Scoring an open distance or a triangle after you land, and uploading the IGC to XContest or DHV-XC.
- Sharing a live track. That part needs a connection.

It does not run on Android. Competition pilots who want a dozen fields on one screen tend to stay with it. A simpler iPhone vario is enough if you only want a beep and a log.
