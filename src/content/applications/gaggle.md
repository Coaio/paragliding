Gaggle is a flight recorder for iPhone and Android, made by Viszen in South Africa. It logs the flight, shows climb and sink with a tone, and shares a live location so other pilots, or someone on the ground, can follow along. Nearby aircraft warnings come through SafeSky.

The free plan is the recorder, the basic instruments, the live track, and those traffic warnings. Paid plans add spoken cues, routes, airspace alerts, a thermal display, groups, and a 2D or 3D replay. The higher plan also adds site forecasts. A paid plan can send the track to XContest or PureTrack, and it can run on Apple Watch or Wear OS.

Gaggle calls the free plan Wingman. That is a plan name inside Gaggle, not the Wingman app.

It is still a phone. Load what you need before you lose signal, and keep a lookout. The log is a backup unless your contest or airspace rules accept it.
