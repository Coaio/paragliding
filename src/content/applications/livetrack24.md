LiveTrack24 is a map of flights in progress. An instrument or a phone app posts your position, and someone on the ground opens the link. Flyskyhy is one of the apps that can send to it. The tracker is not a rescue service. It only shows what the phone managed to upload.

## Before you rely on it

- Start a test track in the car park and confirm it appears on the website.
- Tell one person the link, and agree what they should do if the track stops.
- Remember that a valley with no signal sends nothing. A frozen icon can mean bad coverage, a dead battery, or a landing.

Use it together with whatever local retrieval plan your club already has. A dot on a map is not a substitute for that plan.
