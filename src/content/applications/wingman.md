Wingman is a flight app for iPhone, with a vario that also runs on the Apple Watch by itself. The tone uses the phone's barometer and accelerometer. The map shows simplified airspace and warns when you get close. Waypoints and routes are included, and the flight can be replayed in 3D after you land.

It is the simpler of the iPhone instruments. Pilots who want a fully rebuilt screen, or a competition task with many fields, usually pick Flyskyhy instead.

The app is free to download. Extra features are paid. It does not run on Android.

Try the vario on the ground: lift the phone, check the tone, and read the screen in sun before you trust it in the air.
