XCSoar is a tactical glide computer for gliders and paragliders. It is free, open source, and it does not ask for an account. The same program runs on Android, iPhone, Windows, macOS, Linux, and a Kobo e-reader. Most paraglider pilots meet it on a phone or a tablet, often paired with a Bluetooth vario.

The interface is dense. Plan on an evening of setup before you fly with it.

## What you get

- A moving map with terrain, airspace from OpenAIP and other files, and spoken airspace warnings.
- Task planning for racing and assigned-area tasks, including a task QR code.
- Thermal assistance, and weather overlays such as SkySight, RASP, and satellite pictures when you are online.
- Traffic from FLARM, the Open Glider Network, and SkyLines.
- An IGC log that stays on the device. You can upload it yourself.

It works offline once the maps and airspace are loaded. The project publishes a manual, and that is the way to learn the menus rather than tapping through them at launch.
