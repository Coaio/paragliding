Airtribune is where many competitions publish their public site. An event page holds the pilots, the tasks, the live track, and the scores. National championships and smaller opens use it as well as larger meets.

During a task the track is entertainment and, for a retrieve crew, a way to see who is still in the air. The ranking on the screen can change when a flight is checked. The posted result is the one that stands after the jury, not the one you refreshed at goal.

If you are entering, read the local regulations on that event's page. The software does not replace them.
