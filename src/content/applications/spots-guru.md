A forecast tells you the weather. It does not tell you which hill to drive to. Spots Guru starts from your location, scores nearby sites for paragliding and other wind sports, and lists the best time slots. It is a website that works on a phone and on a computer.

The same atlas covers kitesurfing, surfing, wing foiling, and windsurfing. For free flight, the useful pieces are the thermal map, the flight paths, and a site's wind window.

## How a session is chosen

You set what you fly. The site then looks at wind, and at the other limits you put on a spot, and marks the hours that fit. High-resolution models such as AROME 1.3, ICON-D2, and RRFS CONUS are used for the short range. GFS covers the 16-day outlook. You can compare up to 16 models instead of trusting one.

Live wind comes from open weather-station data, so a good slot on the model can be checked against what the stations are reporting.

## Your own sites

The atlas is shared, which helps on a trip. If you know a launch the atlas has wrong, edit the wind limits yourself. Spots can stay private or be shared with everyone else.

A free account includes the atlas, the stations, hour-by-hour high-resolution data, and the 16-day view. The forecast algorithm on the free plan does not run every day. Premium adds a daily algorithm, updates four times a day, private spots, offline atlas access, and no ads. Check [the pricing page](https://spots.guru/overview) for the current fee.
