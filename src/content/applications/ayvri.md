Ayvri turns a GPS track into a 3D replay over the landscape. Upload an IGC file from a flight and you can watch the line, the height, and where you slowed down. It is a review tool for the evening, not an instrument.

Use it when a flight felt confused. The replay shows whether you left a thermal early, whether the glide was into a valley wind, and where the other option was. It does not show the air you felt, so a smooth line can still have been a rough ride.

XContest and other contests remain the place that scores the flight. Ayvri is the place that lets you look at it.
