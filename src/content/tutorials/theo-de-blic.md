Théo de Blic is an acro pilot. NOVA, the manufacturer he joined in 2020, describes him as a three-time Acro Paragliding World Cup winner (synchro in 2015, then 2017 and 2019) and a three-time Acro Game champion. His own channel calls him a five-time World Cup champion.

The useful part for most pilots is the tutorial series, filmed in the air: ground handling, launch, wingover, full stall, SAT, and SIV. He progresses one maneuver at a time. Start with the ground-handling and launch films before the acro. Treat the overhead and stall work as something to practice with an instructor.
